How to use it

The song plays right here from YouTube - nothing to download. The pinned panel shows the section you are in, the one thing to do, the chords going by, and which holes are friendly (they glow on the map). If the callouts sit a little ahead or behind what you hear, nudge sync with −5 / +5.

Reading the chips: 4B is 4 blow, -5 is 5 draw, and a ' is a half-step bend down. The note names sit on the hole map itself. Friends are neighborhoods, not instructions - hang around them, land on the root, and trust your ear over the clock.
